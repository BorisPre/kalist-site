Frequently asked questions

What is calisthenics?

Calisthenics is bodyweight training: push-ups, pull-ups, dips, squats and core work, then skills such as the muscle-up, handstand, front lever and planche. It needs little equipment and works at a street workout park or at home.

Is KALIST free?

Yes. The free version includes the full movement library, workout tracking, the Journey, four programs and three camera analyses per month. KALIST Premium, a subscription, unlocks all 24 programs, custom workouts and unlimited analyses.

Do I need equipment?

Not to start: many movements need nothing at all. A pull-up bar, parallettes or rings then open up the pulling and balance skills. Every exercise page lists the equipment it uses.

Are my videos uploaded?

No. The coach analyses the camera image directly on your phone. Videos are only recorded if you turn recording on, and they only leave the device if you share them yourself.
